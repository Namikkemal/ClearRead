Privacy Policy for ClearRead
Effective Date: May 2, 2026
1. Introduction
Welcome to ClearRead ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, process, and protect your information when you use the ClearRead mobile application (the "app"). Developed by Muhammet Talha, operating under the brand noadz.dev, ClearRead is designed from the ground up with a strict "offline-first" and "privacy-by-design" philosophy. By downloading, accessing, or using the app, you signify your understanding and acceptance of the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
2. Information Collection and Use
ClearRead is a strictly local, offline application. We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), usage metrics, behavioral analytics, or diagnostic data.

Account Information: We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.
Usage Data: We do not track how long you use the app, which features you interact with, or any other telemetry data.
Document Content: We have absolutely no access to the PDF files, documents, or texts you open within the app. All content remains strictly on your local device.

3. Device Permissions and Local Processing
To provide its core functionality as a document reader, the app requires certain permissions on your device.

Storage Access: On Android 12 and below, the app may request Read External Storage permission. On Android 13+, the app uses the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF), allowing you to grant access only to specific files you choose to open. The app never accesses files you have not explicitly selected. For large files, the app may create temporary local cache files to optimize reading performance (such as fast scrolling). These cache files are not transmitted anywhere and are strictly managed within your device's local file system.

4. No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising
In alignment with our core principles, ClearRead is completely devoid of advertisements and third-party tracking mechanisms.

No Ad Networks: We do not integrate any third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Facebook Audience Network).
No Analytics Trackers: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Mixpanel) to monitor your activity. Your reading habits are completely private.

5. Data Security
Because ClearRead does not collect or transmit any data to remote servers, the security of your documents is inherently tied to the security of your personal device. The security of your documents depends on your device's security. We recommend using standard device security features such as screen locks and encryption.
6. User Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Other Global Privacy Laws)
Various global privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in the United States, grant users specific rights regarding their personal data (e.g., the right to access, delete, or restrict processing). Because ClearRead does not collect or hold any personal data, these requests are inherently fulfilled. There is no data for us to provide, modify, or delete.
7. Children's Privacy
Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Because the app operates entirely offline and collects zero data, it is universally safe and compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global regulations.
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to update or modify our Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Since we do not have your contact information, we cannot notify you of changes via email. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates. Continued use of the app after modifications indicates your acknowledgment of the updated terms.
